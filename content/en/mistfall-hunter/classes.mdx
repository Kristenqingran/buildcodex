Mistfall Hunter currently has six playable classes. Each follows a different combat identity, from durable melee pressure and ranged control to precision attacks, mobility, support and heavy frontline play. This page explains the class system and switching mechanics without ranking the classes.

## How Classes Work in Mistfall Hunter

Steam describes every Mistfall Hunter class as having two weapon identities or stances. Your class determines the combat tools and role available to your hunter, while your chosen weapon path, skills, talents and equipment shape how that class plays during an extraction.

The class summaries below combine confirmed class information with BuildCodex editorial descriptions. They are an overview of how the classes function, not a tier list or balance ranking.

## How Many Classes Are in Mistfall Hunter?

There are currently **six Mistfall Hunter classes**:

- Mercenary
- Sorcerer
- Blackarrow
- Shadowstrix
- Seer
- Withered Knight

## All Mistfall Hunter Classes

The table provides a quick comparison of all six classes. “Guide role” and “playstyle fit” are editorial summaries rather than official balance claims.

### Mercenary

Mercenary is a versatile melee fighter built for direct pressure and defensive reactions. Current guide research identifies **Sword and Shield** and **Hammer** as its weapon identities: Sword and Shield supports blocking, parrying and sustained pressure, while Hammer favors heavier, more committed attacks.

### Sorcerer

Sorcerer is a ranged magic controller built around positioning, space control and deliberate spell setup. Existing guide material describes a **Staff-based** identity with Stardust and Elemental magic paths; both reward maintaining distance, but differ in casting pace and risk.

### Blackarrow

Blackarrow is the free-aim ranged specialist. Its confirmed current identity centers on the **Bow**, using range, elevation, arrow choices and disciplined spacing to pressure targets; that distance only becomes an advantage when shots land.

### Shadowstrix

Shadowstrix is the mobile assassin archetype, using **Dagger** and **Dual Blades** weapon identities. Both support aggressive engagement control, but their attack rhythms differ; mobility lets the class enter, pressure and reposition rather than absorb extended punishment.

### Seer

Seer has the broadest role split in the roster. Current guide research associates its support-oriented **Catalyst** identity with healing, protection and control, while the **Mace** supports a more aggressive close-range style.

### Withered Knight

Withered Knight is a heavy frontline class defined by deliberate attacks, defensive timing and control. Its documented weapon identities are **Greatsword** and **Polearm and Shield**, supporting an offensive heavy-melee route and a more defensive, space-controlling route.

## Can You Change Class in Mistfall Hunter?

**Yes.** Multiple current guides report that you can change class after character creation by speaking to Dew at camp. A same-character switch keeps the character's current level, but class-specific equipment may not work with the new class.

Class changes may require Gyldenblod and trigger a cooldown before another switch. Multiple guides also report a temporary reversion window that lets you return to the previous class without another switch fee.

> **Community guide consensus · Checked August 23, 2026.** Bellring Games has not published a complete official class-switching FAQ. Exact costs, timers and inventory restrictions may change or vary by character level, so check the current information shown in the game before confirming.

### How to Change Class

1. Progress through the opening section until the camp systems are available.
2. Speak to Dew at camp.
3. Store valuable items or class-specific gear before switching.
4. Choose the class-switch option.
5. Review the current Gyldenblod cost, cooldown and restrictions shown in the interface.
6. Select and confirm the new class.

If you cannot switch class, first check whether you still have valuable or class-specific items equipped or in your carried inventory. Current sources do not establish one universal prerequisite quest, level requirement or fixed price.

### Can You Switch Classes After Character Creation?

Yes. Current guides agree that the class selected during character creation is not permanent. Switching through Dew retains the same character level. Some equipment tied to the previous class may become unusable, and the exact handling of equipped items and carried inventory is not fully confirmed, so review the live restrictions before switching.

### What Happens to Skills, Talents and Progression?

Current community reports indicate that some skill, talent or saved-loadout configurations may reset or need to be configured again after a class switch. The exact behavior of talent points, saved builds, quests and other progression is not fully confirmed. Do not assume every configuration will carry over; review your setup after switching.
