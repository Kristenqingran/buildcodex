## The six classes

Mistfall Hunter launches with six distinct classes. Steam describes each class as mastering two weapon stances; the role summaries below are BuildCodex editorial guidance.

| Class | Guide role | Best fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercenary | Durable melee all-rounder | New players learning the extraction loop |
| Sorcerer | Ranged magic and area control | Players who value spacing and setup |
| Blackarrow | Precision ranged pressure | Confident free-aim players |
| Shadowstrix | Mobile assassin | Flanking and aggressive solo play |
| Seer | Support or aggressive hybrid | Coordinated teams and flexible players |
| Withered Knight | Heavy technical frontline | Deliberate melee and defensive timing |

## Mercenary

The clearest starting point for direct melee. Its defensive options make mistakes easier to study, while heavier weapon choices reward commitment and timing.

## Sorcerer

A ranged controller built around positioning. The class can shape crowded fights, but becomes vulnerable when escape and control tools are exhausted.

## Blackarrow

The free-aim specialist. Range is an advantage only when shots land, so tracking, elevation and disciplined spacing matter more than passive safety.

## Shadowstrix

The assassin archetype: enter on your terms, create burst pressure, then reposition. Mobility is a tool rather than extra durability.

## Seer

The broadest role split. Support-oriented play can protect a trio, while the aggressive path trades that identity for close-range pressure.

## Withered Knight

Heavy weapons, defensive timing and deliberate control define this class. It suits players who prefer reading opponents over constant speed.

## Which class should a beginner choose?

**Editorial recommendation:** start with Mercenary if you want the most readable learning experience. Choose the fantasy and mechanics you will practice, not a tier label that may expire after the next patch.
