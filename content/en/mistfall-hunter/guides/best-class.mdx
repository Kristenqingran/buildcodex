> **Community consensus · Updated August 12, 2026.** This ranking combines the supplied public guide research with editorial evaluation. It is not an official balance statement.

## How this ranking works

One list cannot describe every queue. We weigh learning curve, solo agency, team contribution, mechanical demands and how clearly a class recovers from mistakes.

## S Tier — Mercenary

The safest general recommendation. A readable defensive toolkit and direct melee plan make Mercenary effective while a player learns routes, PvE threats and extraction timing.

## S Tier — Seer for coordinated teams

Seer's support value can change an entire trio fight. It ranks lower for players who want a simple solo damage plan, but rises when teammates coordinate around protection and control.

## A Tier — Shadowstrix and Sorcerer

Shadowstrix brings initiation and escape potential; Sorcerer brings ranged control. Both reward strong decisions and punish careless cooldown use.

## A Tier — Withered Knight

Withered Knight offers a powerful heavy-melee identity, but deliberate animations and defensive timing create a steeper first-session learning curve.

## B Tier — Blackarrow for new players

This placement measures accessibility, not ceiling. Free aim makes missed shots costly. Accurate players can move Blackarrow far above this beginner-focused rank.

## Comparison

| Need | Recommendation | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First class | Mercenary | Forgiving, readable fundamentals |
| Coordinated trio | Seer | Team protection and control |
| Aggressive solo play | Shadowstrix | Mobility and engagement control |
| Ranged control | Sorcerer | Space control and pressure |
| Precision challenge | Blackarrow | High reliance on free aim |
| Heavy melee | Withered Knight | Deliberate defense and punish windows |

## Final recommendation

Pick Mercenary when you want the smoothest learning path. Pick Seer for a regular trio. If a specific weapon fantasy makes you want to practice, that motivation is more valuable than a temporary tier position.
