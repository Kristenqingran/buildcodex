## How Ciphers Work

Ciphers are special items that can be taken back to Windrest Camp and deciphered by camp NPCs. The important part is not simply finding someone with a Decipher option — you need to identify the NPC that matches the Cipher’s keywords.

A Cipher’s clue appears near the end of its item description. Those words point toward the correct camp character. Matching the Cipher to the right NPC matters because the correct turn-in can produce better rewards.

## How to Decipher a Cipher

Each Cipher contains specific keywords that point to the correct person or merchant in camp. Before turning it in, inspect the Cipher and note the highlighted words near the end of its description.

The basic process is:

1. Find a Cipher during a run.
2. Inspect it and read the final keywords in its description.
3. Match those keywords to the correct camp NPC.
4. Speak to that NPC and choose Decipher.
5. Select the Cipher and confirm the action to receive the reward.

The key step is finding the right NPC. Multiple camp characters can show a Decipher option, so seeing the button does not mean you are at the correct person.

For example, one guide shows a Bloodbound Cipher with the keywords “Mineral Vein” and “Oathbound.” Those clues lead to the Blacksmith.

Another example uses “Hoarding” and “Haggling,” which point to Pip, the auction-house NPC rather than the normal shopkeeper.

## Rewards & Common Questions

### Why does the correct NPC matter?

Correctly deciphering a Cipher with its matching NPC can award the normal gold payout plus a first-time bonus for that Cipher type. The video example shows a successful Bloodbound Cipher turn-in awarding gold together with an additional item.

If the same Cipher type is found again later, the video reports that it can still be turned in for gold, but the first-time bonus does not repeat.

### Can every NPC decipher a Cipher?

Several camp NPCs may display a Decipher option. The important part is matching the Cipher’s keywords to the correct NPC rather than simply choosing the first character who offers the menu option.

### Where do I find the clue?

Look near the final lines of the Cipher item description. The visible words act as clues to the NPC associated with that Cipher.

### What happens if I choose the wrong NPC?

Player guides warn that using the wrong NPC can result in a worse outcome than correctly matching the Cipher.

### Golden Cipher Event Note

A limited-time Golden Cipher event also rewarded players for correctly deciphering different Cipher types. In the referenced video, milestone progress was shown at 10 and 20 deciphered Ciphers, while first-time correct turn-ins could also award bonus items.

The same event advertised additional in-game and physical prizes. Because this was a time-limited event, its reward pool, milestones and availability should not be treated as permanent Cipher mechanics.
