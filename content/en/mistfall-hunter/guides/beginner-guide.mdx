Your first goal is not a perfect clear or an endgame build. It is to prepare a loadout you can afford to lose, learn how one class handles pressure, and turn a modest haul into permanent progress by extracting successfully.

## What Is Mistfall Hunter?

Mistfall Hunter is a third-person PvPvE extraction ARPG. You enter the Gyldenmist, fight Corroded enemies and potentially rival Gyldhunters, collect gear and resources, then decide when to leave. Loot only becomes useful long-term when you bring it back to camp, so survival and extraction matter as much as winning fights.

The official design leaves room for different approaches. A successful hunt may involve combat, tasks, careful looting or an early exit; defeating every enemy is not the requirement for a productive run.

## What to Do Before Your First Run

### Finish the tutorial and read the live UI

Use the tutorial to learn movement, attacks, dodging, healing, inventory interaction and the current extraction prompts. Live control bindings and item descriptions are safer references than an old video because the game is still receiving regular updates.

### Learn one class and one weapon identity

Do not try to memorise all six classes immediately. Spend a few runs learning the range, recovery time and defensive options of one class. The [classes guide](/mistfall-hunter/classes/) explains each identity without turning the choice into a tier list.

### Give the run one purpose

Choose a small objective before deploying: learn part of the map, complete a task, practise a fight, or extract with a manageable haul. One clear goal makes it easier to recognise when the run is already worth saving.

Advanced affix thresholds, Holy equipment and optimised boss routes can wait. They matter later, but they do not need to occupy your first hour.

## Your First Loadout

Bring equipment that lets you practise the complete combat loop, but treat every deployed item as being at risk. If losing a kit would stop you from playing another run, it is too expensive for a first attempt.

Before matchmaking, check:

- both equipped weapon options and the skills assigned to them
- armour and remaining durability
- healing and any tactical items you actually intend to use
- available inventory space
- whether your talents support the skills you selected

Combat Bags, Equipment Vouchers and Scavenger Squad rewards can provide lower-risk ways to get back into the field. Their tiers and availability may change, so check the current camp interface instead of relying on a fixed farming recipe.

For class-specific loadout directions, continue to [Mistfall Hunter builds](/mistfall-hunter/builds/).

## How Extraction Works

Extraction converts the equipment and resources gathered during a hunt into usable progress at camp. Once your original objective is complete or the value of your haul outweighs the benefit of one more fight, start planning the exit rather than continuing automatically.

Current guides and launch footage consistently show Returner Woodlings, Souls of Return and Returning Chimes as central extraction concepts. However, the exact sequence and available alternatives can vary by map, mode and update. Follow the live objective and map prompts rather than assuming one memorised sequence applies everywhere.

Failing to extract puts carried gear and loot at risk. Recovery states and protected inventory slots have conditional rules that are not fully documented in current official public material, so check the live descriptions for Pip's Pouch, the Keychain and any recovery item before deciding what is safe.

## Early Progression Priorities

After a successful return, move useful items into the Warehouse and prepare the next affordable kit. The Warehouse, camp buildings, character level, equipment and currencies all contribute to progression, but they do not need to be maximised at once.

The Scavenger Squad can return with resources and Combat Bags. The Forge produces equipment, and the official Affix Gem system lets you customise suitable sockets. Weapons and armour do **not** use a traditional system where the same item is raised through `+1`, `+2` or `+10`; stronger equipment usually comes from loot, forging or replacing a piece with a better fit.

There is no verified universal camp upgrade order. Costs, bottlenecks and seasonal rules change, so invest in the facility that solves your current problem instead of following a fixed list from an older build.

## Choosing Your First Class

Choose by the kind of decisions you want to practise: direct melee, defence, ranged precision, spell control, support or high-mobility assassination. Mercenary is a common community recommendation for readable melee fundamentals, but it is not an official “beginner class,” and another identity may fit you better.

Use [all Mistfall Hunter classes](/mistfall-hunter/classes/) to understand how the class system works. If you specifically want rankings for solo, PvP, PvE or coordinated teams, use the separate [Best Class guide](/mistfall-hunter/guides/best-class/).

## Weapons and Gear Basics

Weapon identities are tied to classes, and switching between a class's available weapon options changes its attacks and combat plan. Rarity is only one part of an item: durability, inherent properties, sockets and Affix Gems can all affect whether a piece suits the build.

Affix Gems are installed into compatible sockets; this is the official customisation system. Do not look for an unverified enchantment NPC or a traditional rarity-upgrade menu. The [weapons guide](/mistfall-hunter/weapons/) explains class weapon types, rarity, durability, forging and gems in more detail.

## Solo vs Trio for Beginners

Solo makes every decision your responsibility. It can be useful for learning sound, routes and disengagement, but no teammate can cover a mistake or help control a second threat.

Trio play distributes pressure and lets roles support one another, but it adds communication, shared objectives and team positioning. Staying near enough to help is usually more useful than three players following separate loot routes.

Availability is patch-sensitive. As of the August 2026 official roadmap, additional Brandrgarde Solo modes were still planned for September, so do not assume every region and difficulty offers the same queue options. Check the current matchmaking screen.

## Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

- **Risking an irreplaceable kit while learning controls.** Use equipment whose loss still leaves you able to queue again.
- **Deploying without checking weapons, skills and healing.** A missing tool is hardest to notice after a fight has begun.
- **Treating every rival player as a mandatory battle.** PvP is a threat and an opportunity, not the only objective.
- **Chasing after your goal is complete.** Pursuit spends time, healing and positioning for an uncertain reward.
- **Emptying the Energy bar.** Attacks, skills and defensive movement compete for a limited combat resource.
- **Staying after the haul is worth protecting.** Switch from looting to extraction before one more room becomes one loss too many.
- **Rushing high-risk bosses immediately.** Learn ordinary enemies, map cues and the extraction flow before making a Mist Lord the plan.

## Mistfall Hunter Beginner FAQ

### Is Mistfall Hunter beginner friendly?

Its basic hunt loop can be learned one step at a time, but PvPvE pressure, free aiming, resource loss and extraction decisions create a real learning curve. Treat the first sessions as practice rather than expecting every run to succeed.

### What should I do first in Mistfall Hunter?

Finish the tutorial, learn one class and weapon identity, prepare a replaceable loadout, and make a successful extraction your first practical objective.

### What should I bring into my first run?

Bring functional but replaceable gear, healing you understand, assigned skills and enough free inventory space to learn looting. Avoid risking a prized kit before you understand the map and exit process.

### How does extraction work?

You must complete the current map's extraction flow to bring your haul back to camp. Returner Woodlings, Souls of Return and Returning Chimes are central concepts, but exact steps can differ by map and update; follow the live prompts.

### Should beginners play solo or trio?

Solo is useful for practising independent decisions but has less room for error. Trio offers role support and shared pressure, provided the team communicates. Current queue availability may differ by map and difficulty.

### Which class should a beginner start with?

Start with the class whose range and defensive plan feel easiest for you to read. Mercenary is frequently recommended by community guides, but no official source designates one universally best beginner class.

### What happens if you die before extracting?

Your carried equipment and loot are placed at risk when you fail to extract. Recovery states and protected slots can change what is retained, so verify those conditions in the current inventory and item descriptions rather than assuming every defeat has identical consequences.
