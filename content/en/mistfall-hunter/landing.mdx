Mistfall Hunter is a third-person dark-fantasy PvPvE extraction ARPG from Bellring Games. Enter the Gyldenmist alone or in a three-player squad, fight creatures and rival Gyldhunters, then reach a Returner Woodling before your haul is lost.

Official product facts are separated from BuildCodex recommendations throughout this guide. Build and tier advice is editorial and will change as balance patches reshape the hunt.
