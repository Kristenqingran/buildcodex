Every build direction on this page is grounded in each class's confirmed role identity — melee all-rounder, ranged controller, precision pressure, mobile assassin, support hybrid or heavy technical frontline. Use these pages to narrow your choices before committing playtime.

## What defines a build in Mistfall Hunter

Each class masters two weapon stances. A build direction answers:

- **Which weapon stance to lead with** — the primary stance shapes your core loop and threat range
- **Which role archetype fits your playstyle** — aggressive, control, support or technical
- **How to approach the extraction loop** — PvE threat management, positioning and exit planning

Gear and talent specifics vary by session and patch. This page gives directional guidance; follow individual class pages for tested loadouts as they are published.

## Mercenary — Durable Melee All-Rounder

**Build direction:** Prioritise the heavier of the two weapon stances for maximum mistake tolerance. A defensive-first approach lets you study extraction routes without frequent early extractions.

**Best for:** Players new to the extraction loop, or anyone who wants a readable default plan.

[Explore Mercenary class details →](/mistfall-hunter/classes/)

## Sorcerer — Ranged Magic and Area Control

**Build direction:** Lead with the stance that enables positioning and zone control. Accept that extended melee range eliminates your primary advantage — keep spacing as a conscious priority.

**Best for:** Players who prefer deliberate setup, map control and spatial pressure over raw burst.

[Explore Sorcerer class details →](/mistfall-hunter/classes/)

## Blackarrow — Precision Ranged Pressure

**Build direction:** Free-aim is the defining mechanical demand. Consistency in tracking and target selection outweighs passive safety from cover. Lead with the stance that rewards your accuracy.

**Best for:** Confident free-aim players who can maintain disciplined spacing under pressure.

[Explore Blackarrow class details →](/mistfall-hunter/classes/)

## Shadowstrix — Mobile Assassin

**Build direction:** Engagement control and repositioning are core tools, not extra durability. Lead with the stance that enables fast entry and exit — your build should maximise burst windows rather than extended trades.

**Best for:** Aggressive solo players who prefer flanking, flanking pressure and selfsufficient gameplay.

[Explore Shadowstrix class details →](/mistfall-hunter/classes/)

## Seer — Support or Aggressive Hybrid

**Build direction:** Two distinct paths exist. Support-oriented builds focus on team protection and control. Aggressive builds trade that identity for close-range pressure. Choose your stance based on your regular team composition.

**Best for:** Coordinated teams that can build around protection, and flexible players who want a high-ceiling hybrid.

[Explore Seer class details →](/mistfall-hunter/classes/)

## Withered Knight — Heavy Technical Frontline

**Build direction:** Deliberate animations and defensive timing define this class. Lead with the heaviest stance and build around punish windows rather than speed or passive defence.

**Best for:** Players who enjoy reading opponents, timing punish opportunities and controlling space through presence rather than mobility.

[Explore Withered Knight class details →](/mistfall-hunter/classes/)

## Choosing your first build

| Priority | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Easiest learning curve | Mercenary — readable fundamentals and forgiving mistakes |
| Team coordination | Seer (support path) — protection and control value |
| Aggressive solo play | Shadowstrix — mobility and engagement control |
| Ranged control | Sorcerer — spatial pressure and zone shaping |
| Precision challenge | Blackarrow — rewards accurate free-aim |
| Heavy melee presence | Withered Knight — deliberate timing and punishment |
