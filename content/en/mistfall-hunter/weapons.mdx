Mistfall Hunter weapons are tied to class identity: each playable class has its own compatible weapon families, combat rhythm and skills. Across the official material and current catalogs reviewed for this guide, **10 weapon types are currently verified as playable**. That is a source-checked working count, not a fixed total officially published by Bellring Games.

This guide covers the current weapon system without treating internal, training, test or announced items as obtainable gear. In particular, Blackarrow's Javelin is announced and in development, but is not included in the current playable list.

## Mistfall Hunter Weapons Overview

Three terms are easy to confuse:

- A **weapon type** is a class-compatible family such as Hammer, Bow or Catalyst.
- A **named weapon** is a particular item within a family, such as the Legendary hammer Skullcrusher.
- **Rarity** is the item's gear tier. Two items can share a weapon type while having different names and rarities.

The number of database entities is therefore not the number of currently obtainable weapon types. BuildCodex does not present scraped test records or internal entries as an official “all weapons” count.

## Mistfall Hunter Weapon Types by Class

### Mercenary Weapons

Mercenary uses **Sword and Shield** for a defensive, reactive identity and **Hammer** for heavier, committed attacks and stagger pressure.

### Sorcerer Weapons

Sorcerer's verified weapon type is the **Staff**. Stardust and Elemental describe magic routes within the class; they are not counted here as two separate weapon types.

### Blackarrow Weapons

Blackarrow currently uses the **Bow**, a free-aim ranged weapon built around accuracy and distance management.

The **Javelin** has been announced by Bellring Games and is in development. It is **not currently playable** and is not counted among the playable weapon types on this page.

### Shadowstrix Weapons

Shadowstrix uses **Dagger** and **Dual Blades**. Both serve its mobile assassin identity, with different attack rhythms for entering, pressuring and repositioning.

### Seer Weapons

Seer's **Catalyst** supports its protection, healing and control identity, while the **Mace** supports a more aggressive close-range route.

### Withered Knight Weapons

Withered Knight uses **Greatsword** for deliberate heavy offense and **Polearm and Shield** for defensive timing, reach and space control.

## Weapon Rarity Explained

The currently documented weapon rarity ladder is:

1. Damaged
2. Common
3. Rare
4. Excellent
5. Epic
6. Legendary
7. Holy

**Legendary is a formal rarity, but it is not the highest tier.** Holy sits above Legendary in the current system. Prismatic records seen in some data sources are not treated as a currently obtainable rarity here.

Weapons also have durability. Official system material states that when durability reaches zero, socketed Affix Gems stop providing their effects until the equipment is restored. Exact durability values vary by item and are not reproduced here.

## Legendary vs Holy Weapons

Legendary weapons are high-rarity named gear. Holy Weapons occupy a higher tier and are distinguished by **exclusive gameplay affixes** intended to enable different builds or alter how abilities work, rather than merely increasing a number.

That distinction matters: “Legendary weapon” is not just a community nickname, but Legendary should not be described as the top rarity while Holy gear exists above it.

## How to Get Weapons

Weapons can be found as loot during extraction runs. The **Forge** is also part of weapon acquisition and gear progression, letting players use gathered resources within the camp equipment loop.

Official material links Holy Weapons to map bosses, with bosses associated with particular Holy rewards. This page does not provide a fixed drop table because a complete, current boss-to-weapon mapping has not been verified.

## Can You Upgrade Weapons?

Mistfall Hunter does not use a confirmed traditional system where the same weapon advances through `+1`, `+2` or `+10` to raise its rarity. In practice, weapon progression comes from extraction loot, the Forge and replacing gear with a stronger or higher-rarity named weapon.

“Upgrade” is therefore best understood as improving your loadout through acquisition, forging and replacement—not promising that one item can be pushed up every rarity tier.

## How Weapon Affixes and Gems Work

Mistfall Hunter's documented customization system uses **Affix Gems and sockets**. Socketed gems add affix effects to compatible gear, and those effects depend on the gem and equipment involved.

This is the closest match for searches asking how to “enchant” a weapon. There is no verified basis here for a separate enchant NPC or enchant menu, so this guide does not invent one. Check the item's sockets and the current Affix Gem interface instead.

## How to Switch Weapons

Weapon use is class-restricted: a hunter equips weapons supported by that class. Where the class supports two equipped weapon identities, the player can switch between those weapon sets during combat and build skills around the two options.

Use the control prompt displayed by the current game client. This guide does not state a default keyboard key, controller button, cooldown or unlock requirement because those details are not reliably confirmed across the reviewed sources and may differ by control scheme or version.

## Holy Weapons

Holy Weapons are endgame-tier weapons above Legendary. Official material describes them as carrying exclusive gameplay affixes and being linked to map-boss drops, making them chase items that can open different build possibilities.

This is only a system overview. A complete Holy Weapon list, exact stats and full boss-drop mapping are not sufficiently verified for publication here.

## Mistfall Hunter Weapons FAQ

### How many weapon types are currently playable?

BuildCodex has verified 10 currently playable weapon types across the sources reviewed for this guide. This is not presented as a fixed official total from Bellring Games, and the announced Blackarrow Javelin is not included.

### Does every class have two weapons?

Most current class identities are described through two weapon options, but not every route should be counted as a separate weapon type. Sorcerer's Stardust and Elemental paths, for example, use Staff magic rather than two independently counted weapon families. Blackarrow's second announced weapon, Javelin, is still in development.

### Can weapons be upgraded?

There is no confirmed traditional `+1` to `+10` rarity-upgrade path for the same weapon. Players improve their weapon loadout through loot, the Forge and replacing gear with stronger or higher-rarity items.

### How do you enchant a weapon?

Use the documented Affix Gem and socket system. BuildCodex has not verified a separate enchant NPC or enchant menu.

### What are Holy Weapons?

Holy Weapons are a tier above Legendary with exclusive gameplay affixes. Official material connects their acquisition to map bosses.

### Is Legendary the highest rarity?

No. Legendary is a formal weapon rarity, but Holy is positioned above it in the current rarity system.

### Is the Blackarrow Javelin available?

No. The Javelin has been announced and is in development, but it is not currently playable and is not counted in this guide's playable weapon list.
