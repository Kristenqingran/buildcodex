# Mistfall Hunter source audit

## First-party facts

Steam identifies Mistfall Hunter as a third-person dark-fantasy PvPvE extraction ARPG developed by Bellring Games and published by Skystone Games. It lists a July 29, 2026 release date, solo and three-player squad play, six classes, two weapon stances per class, free-aim combat, talent trees, gem affixes, and a risk-of-loss extraction loop. These claims may be presented as product facts with Steam attribution.

The six class names used in current content are Mercenary, Sorcerer, Blackarrow, Shadowstrix, Seer, and Withered Knight. Weapon pairings and playstyle details are cross-checked against the supplied research set, but must be labeled as guide summaries unless a first-party page confirms the detail.

## Editorial and community material

`Classes.md`, `Builds.md`, `Weapons.md`, and `beginerGuide.md` contain third-party articles and video transcripts. They are research inputs, not copy-ready sources. Rankings, ease-of-use judgments, matchup claims, build advice, and weapon recommendations derived from them are labeled editorial or community consensus.

No unsupported damage number, drop rate, launch platform, or balance assertion is promoted to an official fact. Rankings are dated and explicitly subject to patches.
